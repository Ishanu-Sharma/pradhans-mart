Cancellation and refund policy

In effect from {{biz.policyEffectiveDate}}.

You pay when your order arrives, so in most cases there is nothing to refund: you simply do not pay for what you cancel or refuse.

Cancelling your order

  • Before we start packing: open your order on the tracking page and press "Cancel this order". It is cancelled immediately at no cost.
  • After packing has started: contact support at {{biz.supportPhone}}. If the rider has not left, we will cancel it at no cost.
  • When the rider arrives: you can refuse the whole order. You pay nothing.

There is no cancellation fee at any stage.

When we cancel

We may cancel an order if items are out of stock, the address is outside our delivery area, or the rider cannot reach you. We will show the cancellation on the tracking page. You pay nothing.

Checking items at the door

Please check your items when they arrive. You can refuse any item that is damaged, past its best-before or expiry date, or not what you ordered. You pay only for the items you keep.

Problems found after delivery

If you find a damaged, expired, wrong or missing item after the rider has left, tell us within {{reportWithinHours}} hours of delivery. Write to {{biz.supportEmail}} or call {{biz.supportPhone}} with your order ID. A photo helps but is not required for a missing item.

Once we confirm the problem, you can choose a replacement on your next order or a refund for that item, including the delivery fee if the whole order was affected.

How refunds are paid

  • If you paid by UPI, we refund to the same UPI ID.
  • If you paid in cash, we refund by UPI to an ID you give us, or in cash on your next delivery if you prefer.

We send the refund within {{refundWithinDays}} days of confirming the problem. We charge no fees and make no deductions.

What we cannot take back

For hygiene and food safety, we cannot take back items that have been opened or used, unless they were faulty or not what you ordered.
