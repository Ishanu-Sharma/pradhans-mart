How long does delivery take?

It depends on how far you are from the store and how many orders are waiting. We do not promise a fixed number of minutes. Instead, the tracking page shows each step as it happens and, once the rider leaves, how far away they are.

What does delivery cost?

Delivery costs ₹{{deliveryFee}}. It is free when the items in your cart add up to ₹{{freeDeliveryFrom}} or more. There are no packing, handling, platform or late-night charges. Item prices are the prices printed in the shop and include GST.

How do I pay?

You pay the rider when your order arrives, either in cash or by UPI. Nothing is charged when you place the order.

Can I cancel or return something?

You can cancel from the tracking page until we start packing. At the door you can refuse any item that is damaged, expired or not what you ordered, and you pay only for what you keep. The cancellation and refund policy has the details.

How do you decide which brands to sell?

We sell only brands that were founded in India and are owned by an Indian company or cooperative. If you think a product does not meet this rule, write to us through the contact page and we will check it.

What do you do with my details?

We ask only for what we need to deliver: your name, mobile number and address. You can download or delete your data at any time from the your data page. The privacy policy explains the rest.
