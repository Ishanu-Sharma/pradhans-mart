Product photos

Every product photo was taken by contributors to Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts and Open Products Facts, who photograph real packs in shops and homes. The photos are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution ShareAlike licence. As those projects ask, each credit below links to the product's page, where the contributors are listed.

We resized each photo and placed it on a white square. Those adapted versions are shared under the same licence. The licence covers the photographs only. Brand names and pack designs belong to their owners and are shown to identify the products we sell.
