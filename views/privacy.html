3. What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we do not build a profile of you.
  • We do not send marketing emails or messages unless you ask for them.

4. Who sees your data

  • The store that packs your order sees the items, the amount and your pincode. It does not see your name, number or full address.
  • Delivery partners offered your order see the store, your street and pincode. The partner who accepts it then sees your name, mobile number, full address and, if you shared it, your location, for that order only.
  • Our admin team can see orders and accounts to run the service, approve partners and help with problems.
  • Our hosting provider ({{biz.hostingProvider}}) stores the data on our behalf and may not use it for anything else.
  • Government authorities, only when a law requires us to share it.

While your order is out for delivery, you see the delivery partner's first name and their distance from you. We do not store the delivery partner's location. It is held in memory only while the delivery is in progress.

5. Third-party code on this site

This site loads no third-party scripts, software development kits, fonts, maps, analytics or advertising tags. Every file comes from our own server, and our security settings tell your browser to block code from anywhere else. Fonts are the ones already installed on your device.

6. How we protect it

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Only the store, the delivery partner and the admin team involved can see order details. Partner and admin sign-ins expire after {{sessionHours}} hours. Customer sign-ins expire after {{customerSessionDays}} days. We collect as little as possible and delete addresses on a fixed schedule, so there is less to protect. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

7. Children

Our service is for adults. You must be 18 or older to place an order, and you confirm this at checkout. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us their details, write to {{biz.privacyEmail}} and we will delete them.

8. Your rights and how to use them

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • have it corrected, completed or updated;
  • have it deleted, unless a law requires us to keep it;
  • withdraw consent you gave us, as easily as you gave it;
  • nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or become unable to;
  • have your complaints heard.

To download or delete your data yourself, use the your data page, or delete your account from your account page. Both take effect immediately. To stop offer emails, use the unsubscribe link in any email. For correction, nomination or anything else, write to {{biz.privacyEmail}}.
